About Jollof

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish cooked in a rich tomato and pepper sauce. It is served at parties, weddings and family meals.

Ingredients

  • Parboiled rice
  • Tomatoes, peppers and onions
  • Vegetable oil
  • Curry, thyme and bay leaf
  • Stock and salt
